Etsy Fees in the UK: What a £80 Order Really Costs

An £80 order on Etsy in the UK is a useful size to test the real cost of selling there. The headline rate most sellers quote is 6.5% — but the total cost of a sale is never just the headline rate, and at £80 the fees add up to real money.

On an £80 order, the transaction fee is 6.5% of the price: £5.20. Then payment processing adds 4% plus £0.20: £3.40. Add the £0.20 listing fee and the full Etsy cost is £8.80, leaving you £71.20 before the cost of the product, packaging, and postage. There is no monthly subscription for a basic Etsy shop, which is why Etsy feels cheap at low volume: you pay nothing until you sell. Note that these are the standard UK rates — the regulatory operating fee of 0.48% applies in the UK on top for some sellers, so check your own statements before you price.

How does that compare with your own store? Shopify's Basic plan in the UK costs £25 a month, and each order costs 2% plus 25p through Shopify Payments: £1.85 on an £80 order. That is £6.95 less per order than Etsy's £8.80. Divide the £25 monthly fee by that £6.95 saving and the crossover sits at roughly four orders a month. Sell more than four £80 orders a month and your own store starts winning on fees alone — before you count the control over branding, customer data, and repeat buyers that a marketplace never gives you.

The honest answer is not "Etsy is expensive" or "Shopify is always better". It depends on volume and order size. Under four orders a month, Etsy's per-order fees are cheaper than paying a monthly subscription, because the subscription is charged whether you sell or not. Above that, the subscription pays for itself — and the bigger your average order, the faster it pays, because Etsy's percentage fees grow with every pound while Shopify's monthly fee stays flat.
